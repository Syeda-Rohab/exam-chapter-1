Yeh Kitab Jin Roles Ki Training Deti Hai

Chapter Explanation — Roman Urdu (Paragraph-wise)

Shuruaati Hissa

Para 1:

Aajkal AI ki duniya mein bohat saare naye job naam ban rahe hain. Lekin ye naam confuse karne wale hain kyun ke zyada tar sab aik hi cheez ke different levels hain. Ye chapter aik map ki tarah hai jo dikhata hai ke ye kitab aapko kis role tak kitna le jayegi.

Para 2:

Ye kitab un naye jobs ko define karti hai jo is liye bani hain kyun ke companies ab AI Workers banati, chalati aur control karti hain. Har role ke saath ek honest baat batayi gayi hai — kitab wahan tak sikhati hai ya nahi. Naam se zyada ye "kitna sikhati hai" wali baat important hai.

Para 3:

Har banda pehle "Foundations" se shuru karta hai — ye basic cheezein hain jo har kisi ko chahiye. Isi level par 2 raaste hote hain: Mode 1 (agent ko apna kaam karwane ke liye use karna) aur Mode 2 (khud AI Workers banana). Mode 2 mein hi asal job titles aate hain.

Para 4:

(Note): Agar aapko "Digital FTE", "SKILL.md" jaisi terms samajh nahi aa rahin to pehle Thesis aur Glossary parh lein, ye chapter unko pehle se pata maan kar chalta hai.

Baseline Section

Para 5 (Foundations):

Har banda ek jaise shuru karta hai — sirf browser mein, kuch install kiye baghair. Yahan sikhaya jata hai: prompt kaise likhein, khud code likhne ki bajaye AI se code karwana kaise, skills/connectors kya hain, aur AI ke zamane mein sochna kaise hai. Ye koi job title nahi deta, bas basic base hai jispar sab kuch khara hai.

Para 6 (Mode 1 Practitioner):

Isi base par aap AI agent se apna kaam karwate hain — sochna, likhna, code karna, plan banana. Ye Claude Code (engineers ke liye) ya Claude Cowork (non-coders ke liye) se sikhaya jata hai. Ye naya job title nahi deta, bas jo job aap ke paas hai usmein aapko behtar bana deta hai.

Generalist Core (4 Main Roles)

Para 7:

Ye 4 main roles ek line ki tarah chalte hain: pehle kya banana hai decide hota hai (Outcome Architect), phir banaya jata hai (Digital FTE Builder), phir system design hota hai (AI-Native Company Architect), phir chalaya jata hai (Cloud AI Engineer). Agar yehi kaam client ki company mein ja kar karein to usay Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) kehte hain.

Para 8 (Outcome Architect):

Kaam 3 hisson mein bata hai: kya karna hai (intent), kaise karna hai (execution), aur check karna (verification). Ye role decide karta hai AI Worker se kya karwana hai aur spec (likhit plan) banata hai. Simple lafzon mein — ye "kya aur kyun" ka jawab deta hai, Builder "kaise" ka jawab deta hai baad mein.

Para 9 (Digital FTE Builder):

Market isay "AI Engineer" kehti hai, ek aam sa naam. Lekin is kitab mein isay "Digital FTE Builder" kehte hain kyun ke ye asal mein wo insaan hai jo AI Worker banata hai — jispar puri company khadi hoti hai. Ye is kitab ka main target student hai — spec likhna, SKILL banana, evaluation, sab yahan sikhaya jata hai.

Para 10 (AI-Native Company Architect):

Ye insaan single Worker nahi banata, balke puri company design karta hai — sab Workers, management, aur unko connect karne wala system. Kitab isay pura sikhati hai, aur is par ek 5-quarter wala certification bhi hai.

Para 11 (Cloud AI Engineer):

Worker banana adha kaam hai, use theek se chalana doosra half hai. Ye role real cloud servers par sab kuch deploy aur run karta hai (jaise Azure, Kubernetes). Yahan prototype se asal working company banti hai.

2 Special Roles

Para 12 (Subject Matter Expert):

Ye ek accountant, lawyer, ya koi bhi expert ho sakta hai jo apna knowledge/experience SKILL.md file mein likh deta hai, taake AI Worker usay use kar sake. Market is role ko often bhool jati hai kyun ke wo sochti hai AI sirf engineers ka kaam hai. Lekin ye kitab isay bhi ek zaroori skill maanti hai.

Para 13 (FDE — kya karta hai):

Normal engineer office mein baith kar product banata hai, customer se milta bhi nahi. FDE ka ulta hota hai — wo khud customer ke pass jata hai, unki asal problem dekhta hai, aur wahin on-site solution banata hai. Sirf demo nahi, asal chalta hua software.

Para 14 (Doctor misaal):

Jaise ek doctor door se files dekh kar advice deta hai, aur doosra khud room mein baith kar patient ko check karta hai — FDE wo doosra wala doctor hai jo khud maujood hokar kaam karta hai.

Para 15 (History):

Palantir company ne sabse pehle 2010s mein ye role banaya tha (naam tha "Deltas"). Unke customers (governments) ko aise logon ki zaroorat thi jo khud on-site aa kar unke masle hal karein.

Para 16 (Farq):

Normal developer ek feature banata hai jo sab customers ko milta hai. FDE ek hi customer ke liye jo bhi chahiye wo sab banata hai. Ye kaam bilkul startup ke founder jaisa hota hai.

Para 17 (FDE vs Solutions Architect):

Solutions Architect sirf salah deta hai aur demo banata hai — deal hone ke baad uska kaam khatam. FDE wahan se shuru hota hai — customer ke real data par asal software banata hai aur tab tak rehta hai jab tak faida na mile.

Para 18 (John Deere Example):

OpenAI ne John Deere (ek purani farming company) ke saath mil kar kaam kiya. Har farmer ke data se AI ne bataya kitni kam dawa (chemical) chahiye. Result: 70% kam chemical use hua, aur customers ka engagement 6 guna badh gaya.

Para 19 (Kyun sab FDE chahte hain):

FDE jobs 2025 mein 800% badh gayi hain. Salesforce aur OpenAI ne bhi apni teams banayi hain. Wajah ye hai ke 95% AI projects fail ho jate hain kyun ke wo company ke real messy system mein fit nahi hote. FDE isi problem ko solve karta hai.

Para 20 (Vendor lock-in problem):

Masla ye hai ke har company ka FDE sirf usi company ke tools use karta hai (jaise Palantir ka FDE sirf Palantir use karega). Baad mein customer chahe to bhi doosri company switch nahi kar sakta — jaise ek plumber jo sirf ek hi brand ki pipe lagata ho.

Para 21 (Ye kitab kya sikhati hai):

Ye kitab aisa FDE banati hai jo kisi bhi company ke tools se bandha na ho — vendor-neutral. Aap chahen to koi bhi behtar tool aane par switch kar sakte hain. Farak sirf itna hai ke normal FDE company free deti hai (kyun ke wo lock-in se kamati hai), lekin ye vendor-neutral FDE ko client khud pay karta hai — badle mein azadi milti hai.

Connector aur Plugin Engineer

Para 22:

Worker banane se pehle ek aur zaroori kaam hai — wo tools banana jo AI agent use kar sake. Market isay 5 alag naam deti hai lekin ye asal mein ek hi kaam hai: Connector (chat apps jaise claude.ai ke liye tool banana) aur Plugin (coding agents jaise Claude Code ke liye tool banana). Dono ke peeche ek hi cheez hoti hai: MCP server. Isi liye ye dono kitab mein saath sikhaye jate hain.

Supporting Roles

Para 23 (Intro):

Har team ko aise log chahiye jo kaam check karein, rules banayein, aur zimmedari lein. Neeche wale 3 roles yehi karte hain.

Para 24 (Evals Engineer):

Jaise gaari ko crash-test kiye baghair market mein nahi bhejte, waise AI Worker ko bhi live jane se pehle test karna zaroori hai — sahi jawab deta hai ya nahi, galat cheez par acha fail hota hai ya nahi. Ye har chapter mein sikhaya jata hai, akhri mein add nahi kiya jata.

Para 25 (Governance Officer):

Jaise company mein har employee ki limit hoti hai (junior sirf $500 tak approve kar sakta hai), waise AI Worker ki bhi limits honi chahiye. Ye role decide karta hai AI khud kya kar sakta hai aur kab insaan ki ijazat chahiye. Ye laws (jaise privacy rules) ko bhi dekhta hai.

Para 26 (Supervisor):

Jab AI Worker koi kaam kare (jaise contract banaye), to kisi insaan ko uski zimmedari leni hoti hai. Ye Supervisor hai — jo work check karta hai aur jiska naam responsibility par hota hai, jaise shift manager team ko dekhta hai.

Jahan Kitab Rukti Hai (Scope Se Bahar)

Para 27 (LLMOps Engineer):

Model ko fine-tune karna sikhaya jata hai, lekin sirf tab jab baaki sab tareeqe (prompting waghera) kaam na karein — ye last option hai. Lekin naya AI model scratch se banana is kitab mein nahi sikhaya jata.

Para 28 (Harness Engineer):

"Harness" wo system hai jo agent ko chalata hai (jaise OpenAI Agents SDK). Kitab aapko ye systems use karna sikhati hai, lekin khud aisa naya system banana nahi sikhati.

Para 29 (AI Data Engineer):

Kitab sirf wo data layer sikhati hai jise AI directly read karta hai (jaise Postgres, MCP). Normal big data/warehouse engineering is kitab ka focus nahi hai.

Closing Line

Para 30:

Sab roles ka pattern hi asli baat hai — AI ka kaam bohat saare roles mein bant gaya hai: Worker banana, unko chalana/control karna, aur unko sikhana. Is map ka maqsad ye hai ke aap dekhen abhi aap kahan khade hain, aur ye kitab aapko wahan se kitna aage le jati hai.

End Summary (Aik Nazar Mein)

  • Sab ka base: Foundations + Mode 1 Practitioner (title nahi, proficiency)
  • Core 4-role pipeline: Outcome Architect → Digital FTE Builder (main graduate) → AI-Native Company Architect → Cloud AI Engineer
  • Client mein embed karo to yehi pipeline Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) banta hai — vendor-neutral training is kitab ki khaasiyat hai
  • 2 unique roles: Subject Matter Expert as Skill Author + FDE (koi aur train nahi karta)
  • Connector/Plugin Engineer: MCP server based, do hosts (chat apps + coding agents) extend karta hai
  • 3 Supporting roles: Evals Engineer (testing), Governance Officer (rules), Supervisor (accountability)
  • 3 roles jo scope se bahar hain: LLMOps (model building), Harness Engineer (runtime building), AI Data Engineer (general data eng)
